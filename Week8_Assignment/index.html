Mickey Mouse

Mickey Mouse is a cartoon character co-created in 1928 by Walt Disney and Ub Iwerks. Mickey is an anthropomorphic mouse who typically wears red shorts, large shoes, and white gloves.

Minnie Mouse

Minerva "Minnie" Mouse is a cartoon character created by Walt Disney and Ub Iwerks. Minnie is an anthropomorphic mouse who is usually portrayed with white gloves, a red or pink bow, a blue, red, or pink polka-dotted dress, white bloomers and yellow low-heeled shoes which occasionally have their own ribbons.

Goofy

Goofy is a cartoon character created by the Walt Disney Company. He is a tall, anthropomorphic dog who typically wears a turtle neck and vest, with pants, shoes, white gloves, and a tall hat originally designed as a rumpled fedora.
