Lasagna Recipe

This is a simple recipe for making delicious lasagna. Making perfect homemade lasagna doesn’t have to be tedious. This top-rated easy lasagna recipe comes together quickly with a relatively short ingredient list.Homemade Lasagna is a family favorite and a great way to feed a crowd.

This is a dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat or vegetable sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. Baked to perfection, this hearty and flavorful dish is a favorite comfort food around the world. Whether prepared with traditional ingredients or creative variations, lasagna is a versatile meal that brings people together at the table.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce to the cooked beef and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  6. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce, followed by a layer of noodles, then a layer of ricotta cheese, and a layer of mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce and mozzarella cheese on top.
  7. Sprinkle grated Parmesan cheese over the top.
  8. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  9. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
