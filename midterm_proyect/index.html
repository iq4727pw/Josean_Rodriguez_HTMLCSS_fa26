A Little About Me

I'm Josean Rodriguez Agosto,I am 28 years old. I was born in Humacao, Puerto Rico in 1998 and lived for 24 years. I currently live in Winona, MN, I met my wife here and I have a 7-month-old daughter.My first university studies were in 2016 at Mech Tech College in Caguas, PR. I hold two diplomas in the automotive field and practiced my profession for 10 years.

During my 10-year career as an automotive technician, I sustained numerous injuries that prevent me from continuing. I developed a deformity in the lumbar region of my back. I have received multiple treatments that have had no effect.I have tried various jobs to support my family, but they always end up straining my lower back and preventing me from continuing. My entire family lives in Puerto Rico, and they always help me with whatever I need to ensure my wife and I are doing well.
